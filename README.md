# Project--ContractLens
ContractLens AI – An AI-Powered Legal Document Analyzer.

In today’s world, legal documents such as contracts, NDAs, and agreements are used everywhere in companies, startups, freelancing, and business collaborations. However, these documents are usually very lengthy and written in complex legal language, which makes them difficult for normal users to understand.

Many people sign contracts without fully understanding important clauses, hidden risks, obligations, or penalties.

To solve this problem, we developed ContractLens AI, which uses Artificial Intelligence and Natural Language Processing to simplify legal contract analysis.

The system allows users to upload a contract PDF, automatically extracts important clauses, summarizes the document, identifies risk levels, and also explains why a clause is important in simple language.

Our project focuses on making legal documents easier, faster, and more understandable for both technical and non-technical users.
